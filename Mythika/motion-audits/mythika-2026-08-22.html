Overall

This codebase is unusually slop-free for its size — no stagger spam, no hover-scale wallpaper, no blur-everything, every effect motivated. The core loop feels juicy where it should (combat) and quiet where it should (menus). What remains are completeness gaps rather than taste problems: the Reduce Motion toggle doesn't reach four ambient loops, dialogs vanish without the exit their entrance promises, tab content snaps, and two timing relationships are inverted (exits outliving enters, a 2.7s linear title crawl).

Issues to address
CriticalJakub

Dialogs disappear instantly after a choreographed entrance

WhatUI.Modal.dismiss() sets visible=false synchronously; the 120ms staged entrance is answered by a hard cut on exit. Elements that just disappear are a checklist-Critical motion gap, and here the asymmetry is glaring because the enter was deliberately tuned.

Why it mattersExits subtler than enters is the rule — but zero exits break continuity entirely: the overlay's world snaps shut instead of dissolving.

Recommended motion100ms opacity-only ghost fade on dismiss (callback fires immediately; only the visual lingers). Under Reduce Motion: cut instantly, as today.

Issues to address
ImportantJheyEmil

Title crawls in over 2.7 seconds, linearly, every visit

WhattitleY advances at a fixed 60px/s from −100 to 60 — about 2.7s of constant-velocity travel with no ease, replayed on every return to the title screen.

Why it mattersDrama earns its duration on a first impression, not on the fifteenth. Un-eased motion reads mechanical, and 2.7s is past any defensible UI budget for repeat visits.

Recommended motion~900ms with an ease-out-cubic settle (fast arrival, soft landing) — keep a longer 1.6s variant only for a brand-new save's first visit if the drama is wanted.

Issues to address
CriticalEmilAccessibility

Four ambient loops ignore the Reduce Motion toggle

WhatThe toggle gates shakes, bursts, flashes and enter animations — but continuous loops keep running: enlightenment aura rings, cultivation aura pulse, fishing bobber ripple, and title particle drift. Looping animations that can't be paused fail the accessibility mandate.

Why it mattersThe setting's name promises the category, not a sample. Vestibular-sensitive players get a toggle that half-works, which is worse than none because trust breaks on discovery.

Recommended motionGate each loop at its render entry: aura pulses freeze at their resting frame, bobber holds center, title particles render static (or skip). One guard clause each — no new systems.

CriticalEmil

Tab content swaps with zero transition

WhatEquipment's three tabs and Bazaar's category lists rebuild staticDraws/buttons and paint the new pane on the next frame. Primary in-scene navigation snapping is a checklist-Critical motion gap.

Why it mattersAt 100s of visits these panels are the app's most-repeated state change; a snap makes each switch feel like a reload rather than a reveal, and users lose spatial continuity between panes.

Recommended motion90ms content fade keyed to a build timestamp (the modal's _openedAt pattern), content-only — chrome and tabs stay put. Skip under Reduce Motion.

ImportantPerformance

shadowBlur = 25 repainted every frame on the title screen

WhatThe MYTHIKA wordmark sets ctx.shadowBlur = 25 before drawing pixel text, each frame, for a completely static ornament. Canvas shadow blur is among the slowest 2D operations; on low-end mobile GPUs it taxes the very 60fps budget the new fps probe measures.

Why it mattersSpeed is non-negotiable — and this is pure cost: zero information, paid per frame, forever, on the first screen every player sees.

Recommended motionBake once: draw the glowing wordmark to an offscreen canvas in enter(), blit with one drawImage per frame. Identical pixels, ~zero cost.

07 · Lens reference summary

Most referenced: Jakub Krehel — Production Polish. Three of the six fixes are his contract applied literally: exits exist, exits are subtler, chrome stays anchored while content transitions. His enter recipe was already followed so faithfully that the remaining work is symmetric.

If you want to lean differently:

— More Emil: after fixing the gaps, consider trimming the banner enter to 250ms and making the scene fade even shorter; nothing else in the codebase wants cutting.

— More Jakub: adopt the build-timestamp fade pattern everywhere content rebuilds (forge refreshes, quest-log updates), not just tabs.

— More Jhey: the reward-ticker and splash-crossfade ideas are the highest-smile-per-line items on the board; do them when you want a fun afternoon, not before the criticals.
