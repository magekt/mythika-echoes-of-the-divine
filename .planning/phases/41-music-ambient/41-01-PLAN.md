# Plan 41-01: Ambient Drone Music System (AUD-01/02/03)

## Design: Slow Detuned Drones + Delay Wash

Each ambient track is defined by:
- **chords**: 4-note arrays (frequencies in Hz), one per chord state
- **chordMs**: crossfade interval between chord states (3.5-7s)
- **cutoff**: low-pass filter frequency (350-800 Hz)
- **feedback**: delay feedback gain (0.60-0.75)
- **detuneCents**: random ± detune per oscillator (5-14 cents)

### Node Graph
```
osc[i] (sine, detuned ±N cents)
  → gain[i] (2s attack ramp to 0.035)
    → biquadFilter (lowpass @ cutoff, Q=1.2)
      → delayDryGain (0.4) → delay (0.7s) → feedbackGain (0.6-0.75) → delay (feedback loop)
      → delayWetGain (0.35) → delay → delayOutput
      → masterGain (0.5)
        → _musicGain (0.04 global)
          → destination
```

### Chord Crossfade
- Every `chordMs` the chord index advances
- Each oscillator's frequency ramps linearly to the next chord's target over 3s
- No clicks: both gain ramps and frequency ramps use linearRampToValueAtTime

## Ambient Track Definitions
| Track | Mood | Chord Pattern | Crossfade | Cutoff | Feedback | Detune |
|-------|------|---------------|-----------|--------|----------|--------|
| title | mystical C major | 4 chords, C/E/G/Bb | 6s | 600 Hz | 0.65 | ±6c |
| ashram | warm D major | 4 chords, D/F#/A/B | 7s | 800 Hz | 0.60 | ±5c |
| combat_dark | tense A minor | 4 chords, A/C/E/G | 4s | 400 Hz | 0.70 | ±12c |
| combat_boss | ominous low | 4 chords, E/G/B/D | 3.5s | 350 Hz | 0.75 | ±14c |

## Scene Wiring
- title.js: `playAmbient('title')` on enter, `stopAmbient()` on leave
- ashram.js: `playAmbient('ashram')` on enter (stays running in scene graph)
- combatScene.js: `playAmbient('combat_dark' or 'combat_boss')` based on zone
- fishing.js: `playAmbient('title')` for calm mood
