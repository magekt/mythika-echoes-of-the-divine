# Plan 41-03: Combat Ambient + SFX Layering (AUD-03)

## Combat Ambient Beds
- **combat_dark** (regular combat): A minor root, 400Hz cutoff, 0.70 feedback, ±12c detune, 4s crossfade
- **combat_boss** (boss fights): E root, 350Hz cutoff, 0.75 feedback, ±14c detune, 3.5s crossfade

## SFX Layering
All 8 combat SFX (attack, crit, skill, hit, heal, shield, dodge, combo) remain separate short-lived oscillator blips that connect directly to `ctx.destination` (not through the ambient chain). This keeps SFX crisp and punchy against the washed ambient bed.
