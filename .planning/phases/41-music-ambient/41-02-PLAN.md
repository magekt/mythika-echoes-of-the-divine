# Plan 41-02: Ashram Ambient Loop (AUD-02)

Ashram scene is the primary hub — calm, warm, welcoming.
The ambient drone uses D major harmony (D/F#/A/B) with:
- 800 Hz lowpass for a warm, open tone
- 0.60 feedback for gentle delay wash
- 7s chord crossfade for very slow harmonic movement
- ±5 cent detune for subtle chorus effect

The loop is seamless because:
1. Oscillators sustain indefinitely (no note-on/off)
2. Chord transitions use 3s linearRamp on frequency
3. Gain envelope: 2s attack on start, 1s release on stop
