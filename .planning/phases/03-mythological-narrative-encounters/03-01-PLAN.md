**Canonical reward routing (REQ-005 — exact precedent, journey.js lines 84-101):**
- `reward.gold` → `Economy.addGold(r.gold)` (negative reduces; clamps at 0)
- `reward.karma` → `Economy.addKarma(r.karma)`
- `reward.divineFragments` → `Economy.addDivineFragments(r.divineFragments)`
- `reward.xp` → `Progression.addPartyXP(r.xp)`
- `reward.prana` → `CultivationSystem.addPrana(r.prana)`
- `reward.cultivationBase` → `CultivationSystem.addCultivationBase(r.cultivationBase)`
- `reward.items` (array of `{name, type, qty}`) → `Economy.addItem(item)` per item (stacking + `QuestSystem.trackCollect` handled inside for consumables/herbs)
- `reward.hp/str/agi/mag/def` → loop `G.state.party` heroes mutating that stat (journey precedent: `h.maxHp += r.hp; h.hp += r.hp`)
- `choice.karma` → `Economy.addKarma(choice.karma)` after reward
- After all grants: `QuestSystem.trackCollect(item.name, item.qty)` for any non-consumable items granted, then `AchievementSystem.check()`
- NEITHER `choose` NOR `setFlag` may assign `G.state.gold`, `G.state.karma`, `G.state.divineFragments`, `G.state.prana`, or `G.state.cultivationBase` directly.

**Marker layers (REQ-004):**
- `G.state.flags['enc_<id>'] = <choiceIndex-marker>` — the durable story marker (key convention matches `boss_svarga`, `titleSeen`)
- `G.state.encounters.seen[<id>] = <choiceIndex-marker>` — bounded completion ledger (prevents re-trigger; NO arrays, only keyed objects)
